About me

Learning the craft, one patient at a time

Hi, I’m Mohammad, a 4th-year dental student at Alexandria University. Passionate about dentistry and enjoy turning what I learn in the classroom into practical clinical skills. Throughout my dental journey, I’ve been working on developing my clinical abilities, learning from every experience, and building a strong foundation for my future career. Particularly interested in growing as a well-rounded clinician who combines precision with good communication and a genuine focus on patient care.
